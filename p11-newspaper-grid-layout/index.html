Grid gives the page its columns

By Saksham Hada

A newspaper page is the classic three-column layout. Here the featured story takes two of those columns, leaving the third for shorter items.

Grid is a good fit because the rows and columns are defined once on the container, and each story is placed into them with a simple span.

Spans stay readable

A story that covers two columns should keep its line length sensible.

Gaps do the spacing

One gap value separates every column and row on the page.

In brief

One line rule

Keep the column count low enough to read comfortably.

Order matters

In-page order should match the order shown on a narrow screen.

Footer note

The footer spans every column, just like the masthead.
